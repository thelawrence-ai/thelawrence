## About me

I am an emerging UI/UX designer and AI developer based in **Chennai, Tamil Nadu, India**. I enjoy turning early ideas into thoughtful interfaces, responsive websites, and practical AI-assisted workflows.

My current focus is learning in public: studying design systems, rebuilding interfaces, exploring generative tools, and shipping projects that combine visual precision with useful technology.

> **Design principle:** Make complex things feel simple, intentional, and human.
